# VoiceAssist -- How to Use

## What is VoiceAssist?

VoiceAssist lets you **talk instead of type**. Press a keyboard shortcut, speak, and your words appear wherever you were typing. It can also **read text aloud** to you.

Everything runs on your computer. No internet needed for dictation or read-aloud.

---

## Installing (Windows)

1. Double-click **VoiceAssist-0.1.0-Windows-Setup.exe**
2. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning -- click **"More info"** then **"Run anyway"** (the app is not signed yet, but it is safe)
3. The installer runs and VoiceAssist opens automatically

## Installing (Mac)

1. Double-click the **.dmg** file
2. Drag VoiceAssist into your Applications folder
3. First time: right-click the app and choose **Open** (bypasses the unsigned app warning)
4. Grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions when asked

---

## How to Dictate (talk-to-type)

1. Click into the app where you want to type (Word, Notepad, a browser, anything)
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Space** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + Space** (Mac)
3. The pill in the top-right corner turns **green** -- start speaking
4. Press the same shortcut again to **stop**
5. Your words appear where your cursor was

**Tips:**
- Speak naturally. VoiceAssist adds punctuation automatically
- It cleans up filler words like "um" and "uh"
- You can also click the **red microphone button** on the floating pill instead of using the keyboard shortcut

---

## How to Read Aloud

1. **Select some text** in any app (highlight it with your mouse)
2. Press **Ctrl + Alt + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Alt + R** (Mac)
3. VoiceAssist reads the selected text aloud
4. Press the same shortcut again to **stop**

You can also click the **speaker icon** on the floating pill.

---

## The Floating Pill

The small bar in the top-right corner of your screen is the **control pill**. You can:

- **Drag it** to move it anywhere on screen
- Click the **microphone** to start/stop dictation
- Click the **speaker** to read selected text aloud
- Click the **menu arrow** to expand it (settings, history, appearance toggle)
- Click **Done** (the checkmark) when dictating to stop and paste

---

## Changing Settings

- **Double-click** the tray icon (bottom-right on Windows, top menu bar on Mac)
- Or expand the pill and click the **gear icon**

In Settings you can change:
- **Voice** -- pick from 9 different voices (male and female, UK and US accents)
- **Reading speed** -- faster or slower read-aloud
- **Spelling locale** -- UK or US English (changes words like colour/color)
- **Keyboard shortcuts** -- change the hotkeys if they clash with other apps

---

## Things to Know

- **First dictation takes a moment** -- the speech model loads on first use (a few seconds)
- **Keep it simple** -- speak one or two sentences at a time for best results
- **It might mishear occasionally** -- it works best in a quiet room, speaking clearly
- **The app sits in your system tray** -- look for the small circle icon. Right-click it for options
- **To quit** -- right-click the tray icon and choose "Quit VoiceAssist"

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Nothing happens when I press the shortcut | Check Settings to make sure the shortcut is not used by another app |
| Text does not appear in my app | Make sure you clicked into the app first before pressing the shortcut |
| Audio sounds odd or robotic | Try a different voice in Settings |
| App will not open | Right-click and choose "Run as administrator" (Windows) or "Open" (Mac) |
